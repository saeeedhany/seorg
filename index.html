How to get the most from it

  1. 1One lesson at a time

    Most take under ten minutes. Each one opens with what you will be able to do and closes with the key points.

  2. 2Answer before you peek

    Try each check question in your head before opening the answer. Recalling is what makes it stick.

  3. 3Practice on a real site

    Your own, a friend's, or one you would like to work on. Search Console is free and shows real data within days.

  4. 4Mark lessons complete

    The path above and the outline fill in as you go, so you always know where to pick up.
